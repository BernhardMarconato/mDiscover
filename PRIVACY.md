# Privacy policy

**mDiscover** does not collect, store, track, or transmit any personal data or telemetry.

## Local network activity
The app discovers and inspects DNS-SD and Bonjour services exclusively on your local network. All network information is processed in-memory on your device and is never sent to external servers.

## Diagnostic logs
Diagnostic logs are stored strictly locally on your device to assist with troubleshooting. They are not uploaded or transmitted.

## Contact
If you have any questions, you can open an issue on the mDiscover repository.
